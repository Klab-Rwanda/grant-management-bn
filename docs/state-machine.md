# Tranche Lifecycle State Machine (`Grant Ledger`)

## Confirmed lifecycle

The MVP lifecycle is reimbursement-only: the startup incurs eligible costs, submits evidence, and receives payment only after institutional verification and committee authorization. Advances and advance reconciliation are out of scope.

The flow has two separate state fields:

- `availability_status`: `LOCKED` | `ACTIVE` | `CLOSED`
- `review_status`: `NOT_STARTED` | `DRAFT` | `SUBMITTED_PENDING_REVIEW` | `CHANGES_REQUESTED` | `VERIFIED_PENDING_APPROVAL` | `APPROVED_FOR_DISBURSEMENT` | `PAYMENT_RECORDED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION` | `DISBURSED_AND_RECONCILED` | `CANCELLED`
- Expense allocations use `DRAFT` | `SUBMITTED` | `APPROVED` | `REJECTED` | `DROPPED`; dropped claims remain in the audit history but are excluded from future submissions.

`PAYMENT_RECORDED_PENDING_CONFIRMATION` represents the interval after institution `PM` records payment and before startup `FIN` or `FDR` confirms receipt. In the current schema this interval is represented by `disbursements.status = RECORDED_BY_INSTITUTION`; tranche state remains `ACTIVE | APPROVED_FOR_DISBURSEMENT` until receipt is confirmed.

### Lifecycle stages

1. **Grant setup:** `PM` configures the program, assigns the startup, and defines tranche amounts, milestones, eligible categories, and any allowed budget variance. The first tranche becomes `ACTIVE | DRAFT`; subsequent tranches remain locked until their prerequisites are satisfied.
2. **Startup execution:** Startup `FIN` records and tags eligible expenses and attaches evidence. `OPS` records operational activity and uploads milestone deliverables.
3. **Pre-submission readiness:** `FIN` runs the readiness check. Missing evidence, over-limit category totals, or missing required milestone deliverables block submission and leave the tranche in `ACTIVE | DRAFT`.
4. **Review request:** `FDR` attests and submits the ready package. The system creates a frozen submission snapshot and locks the tagged entries and evidence.
5. **Institutional verification and approval:** `REV` reviews line items and milestone deliverables. Rejected items require structured reason codes and return the submission to `CHANGES_REQUESTED` for startup remediation. Once verification is complete, `APR` separately authorizes the disbursement.
6. **Off-platform payment:** After approval, the institution executes payment outside the platform.
7. **Proof upload and reconciliation:** `PM` records the executed payment details and bank confirmation. Startup `FIN` or `FDR` separately confirms receipt; the same user cannot perform both sides.
8. **Tranche close and unlock:** The platform validates the payment details, marks the tranche reconciled, records the audit event, and unlocks the next tranche when its configured prerequisites are satisfied.

All lifecycle state changes must go through the centralized state-machine module and write the corresponding audit entry in the same database transaction. Payment reconciliation and any eligible next-tranche unlock must be atomic. Startup expense FX rates are entered manually at allocation time, category variance uses the configured tolerance, and payout amount must equal the authorized amount. Reconciliation remains off-ledger; the startup accountant posts the receipt separately.
